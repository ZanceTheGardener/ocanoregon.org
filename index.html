Our mission

The Oregon Community Asylum Network assists and advocates for people seeking safe haven in the United States through grassroots organizing, education, and sponsorship.

Our goal is a safe and secure landing where asylum seekers can begin growing roots.

Your gift keeps families housed and together

Rent assistance for a single month can approach $2,000. A bond to bring someone home from detention can be $5,000 or more. Every donation goes directly to the people we serve.

Where the need is right now

Many families we support have lost work or a breadwinner because of immigration enforcement in our community. Donations go to rent, utilities, legal fees, and bonds.

Get involved

There are tasks for every schedule, and many don’t require Spanish.

Ways to volunteer

  • Accompany or drive asylees to ICE appointments in Eugene and Portland. We provide an accompaniment workshop first.
  • Pick up food boxes, groceries, and prescriptions, or drive people to collect them.
  • Provide rides for medical, legal and other appointments.
  • Bring a potluck dish to the twice-monthly support group.
  • Share your skills & talents, e.g. household repairs, help a family move, become a school liaison.
